# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which cities are covered in the demo?**  
A: Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy (bidirectional on major routes).

**Q: Is payment real?**  
A: No. Payments are simulated (UPI / Card / Wallet). No money is charged.

**Q: Where is my OTP?**  
A: OTP is sent in-app only (demo code: 123456). It is **never** embedded in the ticket QR.

**Q: Can I track my bus?**  
A: Yes – simulated live map, ETA, and journey timeline under My Tickets → Track.

**Q: How do I raise a complaint?**  
A: Use Complaints in the app or ask the BusMate assistant. Include category and booking ID if possible.

**Q: Is BusMate a general chatbot?**  
A: No. It only answers Booking, Ticket, Journey, Tracking, Policy, Complaint, Feedback, and Greeting intents related to bus travel.
