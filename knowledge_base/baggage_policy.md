# Baggage Policy

## Free Allowance
- **1 piece** of luggage up to **15 kg**.
- **1 cabin bag** up to **7 kg**.

## Excess
- ₹50 per kg for excess weight (operator may vary).
- Oversized items (sports equipment, instruments) require prior approval.

## Prohibited
- Flammable liquids, explosives, illegal substances.
- Valuables should stay in cabin bag; BusMate is not liable for unchecked valuables.

## Lost Baggage
- Report within 24 hours of arrival at destination depot.
- Claims processed after investigation (up to 14 days).
