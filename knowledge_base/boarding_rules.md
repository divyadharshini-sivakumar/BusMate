# Boarding & Conduct Rules

## Boarding
- Arrive at least **20 minutes** before departure.
- Carry a valid government photo ID matching the passenger name on the ticket.
- Show BusMate QR / PDF ticket. QR contains **no OTP or personal secrets** — verification is done against booking records.

## Seat Rules
- Occupied seats marked on the map cannot be selected.
- Some seats may be reserved for women (female preference).
- Changing seats mid-journey only with conductor permission.

## Conduct
- No smoking or alcohol on board.
- Keep volume low after 10 PM on overnight services.
- Follow crew instructions during emergencies.
