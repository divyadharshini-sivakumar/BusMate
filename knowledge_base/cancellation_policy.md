# BusMate Cancellation & Refund Policy

## Free Cancellation Window
- Cancel up to **6 hours** before scheduled departure: **100% refund** (minus ₹20 gateway fee).
- Cancel between **6 and 2 hours** before departure: **50% refund**.
- Less than **2 hours** or no-show: **No refund**.

## How to Cancel
1. Open **My Tickets**.
2. Select the booking → **Cancel**.
3. Refund is credited to original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Special Cases
- Operator cancellation / severe delay (>3 hours): full refund or free rebooking.
- Medical emergencies: email support with certificate for discretionary refund.

## Child & Senior Fares
- Children under 5 (no seat): free, subject to operator rules.
- Children 5–12: 50% of adult fare when sharing seat policies allow.
- Seniors (60+): operator-specific concessions; show ID at boarding.
